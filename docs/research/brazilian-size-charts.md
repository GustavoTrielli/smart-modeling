# Brazilian women's size charts, regular and plus

Research for the ticket "Brazilian women's size charts, regular and plus" on the map "Map: smart-modeling v1 spec". It feeds "Choose the grading approach", which has to pick the size chart that grading works from.

Catalog status, prices and terms were checked on 2026-09-26. The standard itself was not bought. Its contents are described from ABNT's catalog record and announcements, press coverage, and academic papers that cite it. Anything not confirmed that way is marked **unverified**, and assessments are labelled as such.

## Answer in brief

- **One current standard:** [ABNT NBR 16933:2021][abnt] ("Vestuário - Referenciais de medidas do corpo humano - Vestibilidade para mulheres - Biótipos retângulo e colher"). Published 29/11/2021, in force, 34 pages, **R$219,00**. There is no separate plus-size standard: ABNT says the standard's two body shapes include plus size, and the reported size range is **34 to 62**.
- **What it defines:** the wearer's body measurements in cm, from head to ankle. It covers two body shapes, rectangle and spoon, which ABNT says account for about 80% of women. The consultation draft reportedly also had three height bands.
- **Data behind it:** SENAI CETIQT's SizeBR 3D body-scan survey (10,000 people, measured 2012 to 2015). SizeBR data is not open. Access goes through SENAI CETIQT, which publishes no price.
- **Open data:** no Brazilian dataset has the measurements pattern making needs; IBGE's health survey measured only weight and height. The US Army's ANSUR II (93 measurements, 1,986 women, public domain in the US) can fill gaps, but it does not describe Brazilian women.
- **Industry charts** are free or cheap but inconsistent. Size 40 is "P" in the 1995 ABNT chart and "M" in NBR 16933. Plus sizes usually start at 46, with letters such as G1 to G4.
- **Legal:** the STJ (Brazil's Superior Court of Justice) ruled in 2017 and 2018 that ABNT standards are "procedimentos normativos" (normative procedures), which copyright law excludes. ABNT itself said in 2023 that the content of its standards is not protected, only their "forma organizativa" (layout). Embedding NBR 16933's values in our own format therefore looks lawful in Brazil. ABNT's notice and purchase terms say otherwise, though, so a lawyer must confirm before any sale.
- **Recommendation:** buy NBR 16933 now and make it the v1 chart for regular and plus sizes. Transcribe its values into our own schema and credit the source. Get legal sign-off or ABNT's written permission before selling, and include a plus-size sample in the proof plan.

## Terms used here

- **Biotype** (biótipo): a body-shape category defined by how bust, waist, hip and high hip compare. High hip is the girth halfway between waist and hip. A *rectangle* (retângulo) has bust, waist and hip roughly in line. A *spoon* (colher) has hips clearly larger than the bust, with a marked, rounded hip line.
- **Body vs garment measurement:** a size chart lists the body. The pattern adds *ease* on top: extra room for wovens, or less than the body for knits.
- **Height band** (stature band): a height group (short, medium or tall) with its own length measurements.

ABNT's scope line reads: "Esta Norma apresenta um sistema de indicação de medidas femininas para os biótipos tipo retângulo e tipo colher." In English: a system for stating women's measurements for the rectangle and spoon biotypes. ABNT's English abstract adds a classification: "a) covering the upper part of the body or the entire body [...]; or b) covering only the lower body and lower limbs". This reads as a split between garments for the upper or whole body and garments for the lower body. If so, tops and dresses are sized by different key measurements than skirts and pants (unverified).

**Status discrepancy.** The reseller [Target][target] lists the standard as "Cancelada". ABNT's own catalog shows "EM VIGOR" and sells it, and [DIN Media][din] also lists it as current, so Target's label looks wrong. No draft from the textile committee (CB-017) is in ABNT's [national consultation][consulta], so no revision is under public review.

**Contents**, from ABNT statements (the text itself was not read):

- **Measurements.** "A ABNT NBR 16933 sugere dimensões em centímetro para cada biótipo, levando em conta desde o perímetro da cabeça, pescoço, passando pelos ombros, busto, cintura, quadril, costas, coxa, joelho, panturrilha até o tornozelo" ([ABNT via Abit][abit]). In English: centimeter values per biotype for head, neck, shoulders, bust, waist, hip, back, thigh, knee, calf and ankle. The exact list is unverified, including whether it has the lengths used for drafting, such as crotch depth, arm length and bust point.
- **Body, not garment.** "A indicação de tamanho de cada peça de roupa propõe incluir as dimensões em centímetro, de quem veste a roupa" ([ABNT via Abit][abit]). The label states the wearer's body in cm, and ease is left to the pattern maker.
- **Shapes and heights.** Rectangle and spoon, "que representam 80% da população feminina" ([ABNT via Abit][abit]). A 2024 paper reproduces three height bands from the consultation draft: short 1,50-1,57 m, medium 1,58-1,65 m, tall 1,66-1,81 m ([Mitraud et al. 2024][arte21]). An IFRN study also used the medium band ([Dutra and Freire 2024][ifrn]). Whether the published edition kept the bands is unverified.
- **Sizes and labels.** After interviewing ABNT's committee superintendent, O Povo reported that the surveyed women's sizes "variavam do 34 ao 62" ([O Povo 2022][opovo]). Size 40 is "M" in NBR 16933 but "P" in the 1995 chart ([Dutra and Freire 2024][ifrn]). The full number-to-letter mapping is unverified. That study gives size-40, medium-height values: spoon bust 92, waist 74, hip 100 cm; rectangle 92, 78 and 98 cm.
- **Plus size.** "Os biótipos femininos tipo retângulo e colher (abrangendo o plus size) foram designados com base em levantamento antropométrico conduzido pelo Senai Cetiqt, realizado por meio de softwares específicos de simulação do corpo humano e na experiência de profissionais do setor de modelagem e confecção" ([ABNT via Abit][abit]). In English: plus sizes sit inside the same two shapes, which were set from SENAI CETIQT's survey, body-simulation software and the experience of pattern-making professionals. The Associação Brasileira do Plus Size (Brazilian Plus Size Association) took part. Whether plus sizes get their own proportions or larger steps between sizes is unverified.
- **Voluntary, but adopted.** "A norma é voluntária. A confecção ou marca adere se quiser" ([Agência Brasil 2021][agbrasil]): brands adopt it only if they choose. Renner's plus-size brand Ashua moved its chart "às novas normas da ABNT" in 2022 ([Guia JeansWear][gjw-ashua]).
- **Price.** R$166,85 at launch ([Agência Brasil][agbrasil]) and R$219,00 now. Target sells it for R$197,10 (R$137,97 for members) and DIN Media for EUR 93.80 with VAT. ABNT's [small-business channel][abnt-sebrae] advertises a "1/3" discount; the wording is ambiguous, and the discount is unverified for this standard.

## 2. SizeBR, the survey behind the standard

Facts from SENAI CETIQT:

- Design and pre-testing ran 2005 to 2011, and data was collected 2012 to 2015. The survey measured 10,000 volunteers aged 18 and over in 27 cities of 16 states; 64% were women. People were measured in standard underwear, with white-light 3D scanners plus tape, scale and stadiometer, following ISO 20685 ([SENAI CETIQT sheet][cetiqt-sheet]).
- It took 89 scanned and 22 manual measurements. A committee of pattern makers kept about 25 of them for pattern making ([sheet][cetiqt-sheet]).
- Female biotypes found: rectangle about 76%, triangle 8%, hourglass 6%, spoon 5%, inverted triangle 5%. Size charts were built "por faixa etária, gênero, biótipo e região do Brasil" (by age band, gender, biotype and region) ([sheet][cetiqt-sheet]).
- Shapes are classified with published formulas from Lee et al. (2007), using bust, waist, hip and high hip. SENAI authors print all of them ([Bastos and Sabrá 2014][forma-corpo]). For example, a woman is a rectangle if bust and hip differ by under 9.1 cm, bust minus waist is under 22.9 cm, and hip minus waist is under 25.4 cm.
- An earlier grid for Southeast women's upper bodies crossed three height classes (about 153, 161 and 170 cm) with 14 girth sizes in 4 cm bust steps. The resulting 42 standard bodies covered 91.2% of 3,534 women ([Bastos et al. c. 2014][sizebr-paper]).

**Availability.** The data and full charts are unpublished and carry no licence. SENAI CETIQT's stated aim is to "aplicar as tabelas do SIZEBR na adequação de tabelas de medidas utilizadas pelas confecções" (apply the SizeBR charts to adjust manufacturers' charts) ([sheet][cetiqt-sheet]). In practice, that means access through its paid services. Price and terms are unpublished (unverified). Copies of SizeBR charts and of NBR 16933 on document-sharing sites are not licensed sources and were not used here.

**Caveats.** The survey's authors note that sampling was not stratified and that "o quantitativo de mulheres com idade mais avançada e acima do peso é bastante baixo" (few older or overweight women were measured) ([Bastos and Sabrá 2014][forma-corpo]). A 2024 paper reports that the NBR 16933 draft said its survey "considerou apenas mulheres da região sudeste" (included only Southeast women) ([Mitraud et al. 2024][arte21]). That claim is secondary and unverified.

**Assessment.** In SENAI's figures, triangle (8%) is more common than spoon (5%), yet the standard covers spoon; the reason is not documented. The plus end of the chart rests on the thinnest data.

## 3. Industry and academic charts

- **Retailers** publish their own charts, usually listing bust, waist and hip only. Ashua (Renner) runs 44 to 58 with letters M, G, G1 and G2, where 58 is G2 ([Guia JeansWear][gjw-ashua]).
- **Pattern-making books.** Pattern maker and author Marlene Mukai publishes size charts "seguindo as normas técnicas da ABNT" and classifies them: "Do tamanho P (34) ao G (44) é considerado MODINHA. Do tamanho G (46) ao G2 (54 é considerado PLUS SIZE. Do tamanho G3 (56) ao tamanho G4 (62) é considerado PLUS SIZE TAMANHOS ESPECIAIS" ([Mukai][mukai]). In English: 34 to 44 is regular, 46 to 54 is plus, and 56 to 62 is special plus. The page labels both 44 and 46 "G". Her book "Modelagem prática especial plus size" (2020, ISBN 9788592093587) includes plus and special-size charts ([book page][mukai-book], [listing][mukai-isbn]). Its price was not checked.
- **Where plus starts.** Academic and trade sources put the start at 46 ([Rizzi and Jesus 2022][ergodesign]; [Mukai][mukai]). A 2022 paper written before NBR 16933 was published found that "não existem tabelas de medidas específicas voltadas para o segmento plus size" (no plus-specific size charts exist) ([Rizzi and Jesus 2022][ergodesign]).
- **Academic charts.** A 2018 "Tabela de Medidas Associáveis" offers seven biotypes by three heights. It was synthesized from a university course chart using the Lee et al. formulas, not measured ([Longhi and Santos 2018][tma]).

**Assessment.** Numeric sizes run 34 to 62 in steps of 2. Letters run PP to GG for regular sizes and G1 to G4 (or similar) for plus, and each brand maps them to numbers differently. v1 should key its chart on numeric size plus body measurements, and treat letters as a display alias.

## 4. Open datasets

- **IBGE PNS 2019** (national health survey) measured only weight and height, on a subsample aged 15 and over ([IBGE][pns]). The microdata is free; the commercial-use terms are unverified because IBGE's site blocked automated checks.
- **ELSA-Brasil** measured weight, height, waist and hip of 15,105 civil servants aged 35 to 74 ([ELSA paper][elsa]). It is a research cohort with unstated access terms.
- **ANSUR II** (US Army, 2012) has 93 measurements of 1,986 women and 4,082 men ([OPEN Design Lab][ansur-openlab]). It was released in 2017, with clothing designers named as intended users ([US Army][ansur-army]). As a US government work, it is not covered by US copyright ([17 U.S.C. 105][usc105]).

**Assessment.** No open Brazilian dataset can replace a size chart. ANSUR II is commercially safe but describes young US soldiers. Use it only for how measurements relate to each other, for example to fill measurements NBR 16933 lacks or to test body models, not for Brazilian sizes.

## 6. Can we build them into a commercial product?

### Facts

- **Law.** Lei 9.610/1998 ([text][l9610]), art. 8, excludes from copyright "I - as idéias, procedimentos normativos, sistemas, métodos, projetos ou conceitos matemáticos como tais" and "VII - o aproveitamento industrial ou comercial das idéias contidas nas obras". In English: ideas, normative procedures, systems, methods and mathematical concepts, and the industrial or commercial use of ideas contained in works, are not protected. Protection of compilations and databases (art. 7, XIII) covers only their selection or arrangement, and "não abarca os dados ou materiais em si mesmos" (does not cover the data themselves) (§2).
- **STJ, Fourth Panel.** [AgInt no REsp 1.621.370/SP][stj-1621370] (ABNT v. Target, unanimous, 21/06/2018) held: "A Lei nº 9.610/98 excluiu expressamente os procedimentos normativos da proteção dos direitos autorais (artigo 8º)" and "Não se pode negar o uso das normas técnicas àqueles que se proponham à sua produção e comercialização industrial". In English: the law excludes standards from copyright, and no one may be denied their use for industrial production and sale. The ruling relies on the Third Panel's REsp 1.643.007/SP (2017), which noted that bills to protect standards (PLC 1.984/2002 and PLC 2/2006) had failed. That case also let a reseller cite ABNT's marks to show where standards came from ([STJ news][stj-1643007]).
- **Other courts** (secondary reports only). The federal appeals court TRF-3 held in 2014 that consulting firms may copy and distribute ABNT standards ([Migalhas][migalhas]). The STF (Federal Supreme Court) declined ABNT's appeals on procedural grounds, leaving the lower rulings in place: RE 1.158.657/SP in 2018 and ARE 1.189.770 in 2019 ([Conjur 2018][conjur-fachin]; [Conjur 2019][conjur-celso]). Press headlines present the 2019 decision as confirming that standards are not copyrighted, but the STF did not decide the merits.
- **ABNT's position.** A statement of 17/10/2023 reads: "o conteúdo das normas técnicas nos termos da Lei de Direitos Autorais não está protegido pelos direitos autorais, entretanto a forma organizativa das normas é protegida [...]. Assim, a citação do seu conteúdo é permitida, mas o seu compartilhamento, não o é" ([ABNT via Abrava][abnt-comunicado]). In English: content is not protected but layout is, so quoting is allowed and sharing is not. In 2021, ABNT told the press "A norma é cobrada por se tratar de um direito autoral" (the standard is paid for because it is copyrighted) ([Agência Brasil][agbrasil]).
- **Notice.** ABNT standards carry the notice "nenhuma parte desta publicação pode ser reproduzida ou utilizada por qualquer meio [...] sem permissão por escrito da ABNT" (no part may be reproduced or used without ABNT's written permission) ([example][nbr9050]).
- **Purchase terms.** Buyers accept a "termo de uso" at checkout, and electronic standards are read in an online viewer ([ABNT FAQ][abnt-faq]). The current terms are not public. A 2010 third-party copy of ABNT's older NormasNet terms forbids anyone to "criar obras derivadas a partir das informações" (create derivative works from the information) ([copy][normasnet-2010]). Whether today's terms say the same is unverified.

### Assessment

- **Copyright: low risk.** Under the STJ rulings and ABNT's 2023 statement, the numbers in NBR 16933 are unprotected technical content in Brazil. Re-keying them into our own data model looks lawful, provided we copy none of the document's text, figures or table layout and we credit the source.
- **Contract: medium risk, unknown until the terms are read.** The purchase terms may forbid derivative works. Whether a contract can block use of content the law leaves unprotected is a separate legal question.
- **ADR-0001 fit: needs the owner's call.** ADR-0001 asks for licences or terms that allow commercial use. For NBR 16933, the law appears to allow it, but ABNT's notice and terms do not. The owner should decide whether "unprotected by law" satisfies ADR-0001, ideally after a lawyer reads the actual terms, or once ABNT grants written permission. The ABNT catalog lists comercial@abnt.org.br for purchases and cit@abnt.org.br for technical questions.
- **Outside Brazil:** not analyzed.
- **Other sources.** SizeBR data needs a contract with SENAI CETIQT. Book charts are ordinary copyrighted works, where the "normative procedures" exclusion does not apply, so copy them only with permission. ANSUR II is safe to use. The body-shape formulas are methods (art. 8, I), so they are free to use.

### Needs legal confirmation

1. Do ABNT's current purchase terms restrict using NBR 16933's values inside software we sell? If so, are those restrictions enforceable, given art. 8 and the STJ rulings?
2. Does storing the values in our own schema count as copying the "forma organizativa" that ABNT says is protected?
3. May the product say "based on ABNT NBR 16933"? The STJ let a reseller cite ABNT's marks to show where standards came from ([STJ news][stj-1643007]).

## 7. Recommendations

For "Choose the grading approach":

1. Adopt ABNT NBR 16933:2021 as the v1 size chart for regular and plus sizes, keyed by numeric size, biotype and height band. Buy it now (R$219,00, one-off), because the grading choice depends on its actual measurement list and on how values step from size to size.
2. Transcribe the values into our own schema, with a source field on each value. Never ship the PDF, its text or its figures.
3. Have the paid pattern maker check that NBR 16933 has every measurement our drafts need for each garment family. Fill any gaps with the pattern maker's rules or with ANSUR II-based proportions, and mark those values as derived.
4. Use rectangle (76% of women in SizeBR) as the default shape and spoon as the second. Treat other shapes as outside v1 unless the owner wants custom measurements.
5. Include at least one plus-size sample in the proof plan, since the plus end of the chart has the weakest evidence.
6. Get legal confirmation or ABNT's written permission before any sale. Until then, the owner's personal use carries little risk.
7. Approach SENAI CETIQT for a SizeBR licence only if NBR 16933 proves insufficient.

For "Choose the 3D preview engine and body model": the preview body should be built from the same chart rows (size, biotype and height band), so the body model must accept these measurements as input.

## Confidence and gaps

**Solid:** NBR 16933's catalog record, status and price (ABNT's own catalog); that no plus-specific ABNT standard exists; the cancellation dates of related standards; SizeBR's scope, and that its data is not open; the STJ rulings (primary text) and ABNT's 2023 position; ANSUR II's public release.

**Unverified:** what the published NBR 16933 contains, since it was not bought: the full measurement list, letter mapping, height bands and plus-size steps. Also unverified: ABNT's current purchase terms; SizeBR licence terms and price; IBGE's commercial-use terms; whether the small-business discount applies; and the TRF-3 and STF decisions, seen only through legal news sites.
